CK Drive

Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

CK Drive is a personal app, for Android, Windows and the web, for using your own Google Drive. It shows your Drive files, uploads and manages them for you, and warns you before you upload a file that's already in your Drive.

What CK Drive accesses

When you sign in, CK Drive asks Google for permission to see, edit, create and delete your Google Drive files (the drive scope). It needs this to do what the Drive app does: list and search your files, upload, download, rename, move, star, share, and move files to Trash or restore them. It also reads your name, email address, profile photo and storage use so it can show which account is signed in and how much space you've used.

CK Drive does all of this only when you ask it to, with the exception of duplicate checking described below. It never changes or deletes a file unless you choose that action, and moving duplicates to Trash always asks you first.

Duplicate checking

To tell whether a file is already in your Drive, CK Drive keeps a list on your device of the fingerprints (MD5 checksums) Google Drive already has for your files, together with their names and folders. Before an upload, CK Drive works out the fingerprint of the file on your device and compares it with that list. The list never leaves your device. CK Drive refreshes it by asking Google Drive what has changed.

Where your data goes

  • Your data goes only between your device and Google. CK Drive has no servers, no accounts with us, and no copy of your files anywhere else.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

CK Drive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to show and manage your own Drive for you. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared.

What stays on your device

The duplicate-check list, settings like sort order, and copies of files you opened or are uploading (temporary, deleted after use) are kept on your device only. Signing out or uninstalling CK Drive removes them. In the browser, they're kept in that browser's site storage for CK Drive.

Removing access

You can sign out in CK Drive at any time. To remove its access to your Google account completely, go to Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services and remove CK Drive. Your files stay in your Google Drive.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

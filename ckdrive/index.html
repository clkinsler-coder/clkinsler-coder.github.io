CK Drive

A personal app for your own Google Drive, on Android, Windows and the web. It does what the Drive app does, and it notices when a file you're uploading is already in your Drive.

Open CK Drive in the browser

Your Drive, your files

Browse, search, upload, download, share, star, move and rename. Your files stay in your own Google Drive.

No duplicate uploads

Before anything uploads, CK Drive checks whether the same file is already in your Drive, even under a different name.

Clean up copies

Find sets of identical files and move the extra copies to Trash, where you can restore them for 30 days.

Offline & backup

Keep files on your phone to open without a connection, and back up phone folders like Camera, skipping anything already in Drive.

No servers of ours

CK Drive talks only to Google Drive, straight from your device. Nothing goes anywhere else.

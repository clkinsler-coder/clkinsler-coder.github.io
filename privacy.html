CK Password Manager

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

CK Password Manager is a personal password manager made up of an Android app and a Chrome extension. It is built so that your passwords, notes and other vault data can only be read by you.

What the app stores

Your logins, passwords, passkeys, notes, cards and 2FA secrets are kept in a vault on your own devices. The vault is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your master password with Argon2id. Your master password is never stored and never leaves your device. Nobody else — including the developer — can open your vault.

Google Drive sync

If you turn on sync, the app asks Google for permission to use its own hidden app-data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). It uses that folder only to store one file: your encrypted vault, so your phone and browser can stay up to date. The app cannot see, read or change any of your other Google Drive files, and the file it stores can't be read without your master password. You can turn sync off at any time in the app's settings, and remove the app's data from your Google account under Google Account → Security → Third-party apps.

CK Password Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide sync to you, is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone.

Autofill and passkeys

To fill logins, the app looks at the login screen of the app or website you are using (field types and labels) and fills the login you choose. This happens on your device. It is not recorded or sent anywhere.

Website logos

If "Website logos" is turned on, the app downloads each saved website's logo from Google's public icon service. Only the website's name (for example example.com) is sent — never usernames, passwords or anything else from your vault. You can turn this off in settings.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts with us, no servers of ours: your data goes only to your devices and, if you choose, your own Google Drive.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
  • No selling or sharing of any data.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app or removing the extension deletes the vault from that device. To delete the synced copy, turn off sync and remove CK Password Manager's access under Google Account → Security → Third-party apps, which also removes its hidden Drive data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
